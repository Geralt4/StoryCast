What's New in 1.4.1
• Audiobookshelf books now appear right in your Library, in a folder named after your server, and play in the main player instead of a separate player inside Settings
• Every book library on your Audiobookshelf server is included, and a Sync Now button in Settings refreshes it on demand
• The progress bar now follows the current chapter, and playback moves on to the next chapter automatically (you can turn this off in Settings)
• The current chapter's name now shows under the book title, even when a book has cover art
• Stability and reliability improvements
